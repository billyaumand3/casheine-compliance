Delete your Casheine account

Casheine: Daily Dose of Cash (Google Play package com.mercarue.casheine) is operated by Assurance Partners LLC. This is the official account and data-deletion page for the Casheine app.

This page explains how to request deletion of your Casheine: Daily Dose of Cash account and associated data.

Request deletion in the app

Open Casheine and go to Profile → Edit profile → Delete account.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, send the request from the email address registered with Casheine. Use the subject “Casheine account deletion request”. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Request account deletion

What is deleted

After verification, we delete or irreversibly anonymize your Casheine profile and associated identifiers, activity history, reward history, referral information, notification records, and uploaded profile image.

Information that may be retained

Records that must be retained for fraud prevention, security, accounting, dispute resolution, or legal compliance may be retained only for the applicable retention period and then deleted. Retained records are not used for advertising.

For privacy questions, contact assurancepartnersllc@gmail.com.
