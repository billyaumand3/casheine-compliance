Casheine Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This policy describes how Casheine: Daily Dose of Cash handles information when you use the app.

Information we handle

  • Account information, including email address, display name, authentication identifiers, and profile image.
  • App activity, including offers, surveys, rewards, referrals, points, redemptions, and notification interactions.
  • Reward and redemption information, including the selected reward, redemption status, and the recipient details needed to fulfill a reward.
  • Device, network, diagnostic, approximate-location, and advertising identifiers, including IP address and information processed by advertising services.
  • When you choose to use an offer or survey, a pseudonymous Casheine account identifier, IP address, offer details, and completed or reversed reward information needed to prevent fraud and credit your Beans.
  • Messages or information you provide when requesting support or account deletion.

Why we use information

We use information to authenticate users, operate rewards and redemptions, prevent fraud, provide support, deliver notifications, maintain security, measure app performance, and display and measure advertising and third-party offers.

Service providers

The app uses Google Firebase services, Google Sign-In, Unity Ads, and selected offer and survey providers, including Admantum, Wannads, Kiwiwall, and Notik. When you choose an offer or survey, the relevant provider may receive the information needed to deliver, measure, secure, and credit that offer. No cash-out or third-party reward-delivery provider is currently enabled. If one is added in the future, this policy will be updated before information is shared for that feature. Information is shared only as needed to provide the requested feature, advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Security and retention

We use encrypted network connections where supported and retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above or to satisfy legal, accounting, security, and dispute-resolution obligations.

Your choices

You may decline optional device permissions. You can request account and associated-data deletion in the app or on our account deletion page.
