Casheine Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy describes how Casheine: Daily Dose of Cash handles information when you use the app.

Information we handle

  • Account information, including email address, display name, authentication identifiers, and profile image.
  • App activity, including offers, surveys, rewards, referrals, points, redemptions, and notification interactions.
  • Device, network, diagnostic, approximate-location, and advertising identifiers produced from your device or IP address.
  • Messages or information you provide when requesting support or account deletion.

Why we use information

We use information to authenticate users, operate rewards and redemptions, prevent fraud, provide support, deliver notifications, maintain security, measure app performance, and display and measure advertising and third-party offers.

Service providers

The app uses service providers including Google Firebase services, Google Sign-In, Unity Ads, and third-party offer and survey providers. These providers may process device or other data under their own privacy terms. Information is shared only as needed to provide the requested feature, advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Security and retention

We use encrypted network connections where supported and retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above or to satisfy legal, accounting, security, and dispute-resolution obligations.

Your choices

You may decline optional device permissions. You can request account and associated-data deletion in the app or on our account deletion page.

Contact

Questions may be sent to contact@mercaure.com.
